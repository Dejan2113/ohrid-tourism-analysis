# Ohrid Tourism Analysis

## About the project
This project analyzes tourist visits in Ohrid, Macedonia, over a 10-year period (2016–2025). I chose Ohrid because it's the most popular and most visited destination in Macedonia.

## Data
The data comes from **MakStat**, the official website of the State Statistical Office of Macedonia. It covers tourists and nights spent in Ohrid from 2016 to 2025, split by country of origin, month, and type of tourist (domestic or foreign).

## Tools
- **Power BI** – data model, measures, and the interactive dashboard

## Dashboard
The Power BI report has two pages.

**Page 1: Overview**
- Domestic vs. foreign tourists by year (2016–2025)
- Tourists by month, domestic vs. foreign
- Top 10 countries of foreign tourists
- Average length of stay for domestic tourists, foreign tourists, and a selected country

![Overview page](images/overview.png)

**Page 2: Countries**
- A table with nights, tourists, and average nights per tourist for each country

![Countries page](images/countries.png)

Both pages have a **year filter**, so you can look at one year or all years.

## Key findings
1. **COVID-19 and recovery.** In 2020, the number of foreign tourists dropped sharply because of the COVID-19 pandemic, from about 190,000 in 2019 to only 15,000. After that, it grew quickly and reached a new record of about 240,000 in 2025. The number of domestic tourists grew steadily over the whole period and didn't drop in 2020, because people spent their holidays in Macedonia instead of abroad.

2. **Seasons.** August is the busiest month for both domestic and foreign tourists. Domestic tourists come mostly in July and August, while foreign tourists visit over a longer season, from April to October.

3. **Countries.** Turkey sends the most foreign tourists, but they stay less than 2 nights on average. Dutch tourists stay the longest, with 4.68 nights.

4. **Length of stay.** Domestic tourists stay 4.85 nights on average, while foreign tourists stay only 2.36 nights. Domestic tourists stay twice as long.
